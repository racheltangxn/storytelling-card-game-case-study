# 01 — Overview

The project explored how a familiar image-and-clue party-game loop could work across three contexts: people sharing a room, people connected by a call, and players returning asynchronously.

The central engineering challenge was keeping the experience socially expressive while making turn ownership, reconnection, and scoring unambiguous.

## My contribution

- Shaped the product flow and interaction model.
- Designed the state transitions and multiplayer boundaries.
- Implemented and tested scoring, turn handling, rejoin behavior, notifications, and demo-mode persistence.
- Documented the risks involved in moving from a local prototype to a networked product.

## Outcome

The private prototype reached a coherent playable state with a broad automated test suite. The public artifact focuses on the reasoning behind that work rather than distributing the product itself.
